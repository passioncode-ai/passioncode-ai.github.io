03 / WHAT IT SAVES

The estimate is arithmetic
you can check

Hours your team spends on the processes each month, times the share agents can plausibly take over, times what an hour costs you. The share is lower when work is already partly automated, because there is less manual effort left to remove.

hours a week × 4.33×share agents take×loaded hourly cost

Share agents can take over, by how the work runs today
TodayShare
Mostly manual25–50%
Scripts and integrations20–40%
Chat assistants used ad hoc15–35%
Agents already run parts of it10–25%

Example: 40 hours a week of mostly manual work at $50 an hour is 173 hours a month; agents taking 25–50% of it is 43–87 hours, or $2,150–4,350 a month.

An estimate, not a promise. The pilot measures the real number against your own baseline before anything is rolled out.

08 / QUESTIONS

Before you ask

What does it cost?

The tools are free and open source. You pay for the work we do for you — mapping, building, hosting, support — and a commercial license where you need one. The proposal names the price after we understand the processes; there is no fixed package.

Do we have to use your cloud?

No. The workplace can run on your team’s computers, in your own cloud account, or be hosted by PassionCode.ai. Your data and your model accounts stay yours either way.

Which models and agents can we use?

Any. The workplace is agent-agnostic: Claude Code, Kilo Code and Hermes Agent connect to Fabric today, Codex and Cline run inside it, and more are planned. Any agent, MCP server or tool you already run becomes a Fabric-compatible service.

Do the analytics watch our employees?

No. Analytics are about agents and the outcomes of processes: what ran, what it cost, what was approved. Nothing is stealth, and what is measured is visible to the people it concerns.

When do we need a commercial license?

The tools are under the GNU AGPL-3.0. Internal use, and changes you publish under the same license, need nothing. A closed product that includes them, or a modified hosted service whose source you do not publish, needs a commercial license.

What happens to the answers in this form?

We use them only to reply and to prepare a proposal, and keep them no longer than 24 months. The privacy notice has the details.
