PRIVACY NOTICE · VERSION 2026-11-07

Privacy notice

passioncode.ai has no advertising trackers and no cookies, and it writes nothing on your device for analytics. It counts page views and clicks without identifying you (below), and the only personal data it collects is what you send in a request from /business/, through its form or through your coding agent.

This notice is also published in other languages. If a translation differs from this English version, the English version prevails.

Who is responsible

PassionCode.ai, run by Siarhei Sheleh, is the controller of the data described here. Contact: commercial@passioncode.ai or contact@passioncode.ai.

What a request collects

  • What you write: what agents should do for you and, if you choose one, a main goal. A request your coding agent sends can also carry your organization’s size, the agents and tools you use, when you want to start, and the name the agent gives itself.
  • Your work email.
  • From the request itself: the country Cloudflare reports for your connection, your browser’s user-agent string, and a one-way hash of your IP address used to stop abuse. The IP address itself is not stored.

Why, and on what basis

To answer your request and prepare a proposal: steps you ask for before a possible contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), which is why the form has no consent box. The abuse checks rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the form usable (Art. 6(1)(f)). Product news goes only to people who asked for it, and they can withdraw that consent at any time (Art. 6(1)(a)).

Where it is kept

  • Cloudflare, which serves this site, stores the request first in a database located in Western Europe and sends two emails: one to our commercial mailbox and a receipt to you.
  • The PassionCode.ai Platform, our backend hosted by DigitalOcean in Frankfurt, Germany, keeps it for as long as the conversation needs it.

We do not sell the data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train models.

How long

The copy at Cloudflare is deleted 30 days after it reaches our backend; a request that has not reached it yet stays there under the 24-month limit. A request that did not lead to work together is deleted or anonymised 24 months after its last update. A request that became a contract is kept as part of that contract.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, its correction or erasure, a restriction, or object to its use, by writing to commercial@passioncode.ai from the address you used. You can also complain to a data protection authority; in Poland that is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

Site analytics

To see which pages and buttons help visitors, the site counts page views, clicks on its main buttons, how far the home video is played, copies of the setup command, and whether the request form was started and sent. Each event carries only fixed words (the page, a section, a button) and the page's language; never what you type, a URL you came from, your address or an identifier from your device.

The site's own server makes a visitor-of-the-day number from your IP address, your browser's name and a random value that changes every day and is deleted the next day, so the same number cannot be made again or linked across days; your IP address and browser name are not passed on. The counts go to analytics run by PassionCode.ai at analytics.passioncode.ai. The basis is our legitimate interest in improving the site. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing is counted.

In your browser

While you write in the form, your unsent text is kept only in your own browser’s storage so a reload does not lose it; it is removed when the request is sent. A coding agent you use keeps its conversation with you under its own terms, not ours.

The apps

The PassionCode.ai apps run on your computers and do not send your work to us. Fabric Switchboard, Fabric since version 0.3.2 and Fabric Dashboards since version 0.8.19 send anonymous usage counts (Fabric Dashboards also counts errors): counts and kinds only, never names, paths or content. Each product has its own switch: Switchboard’s About window, Fabric’s Settings, and Settings → Privacy in Fabric Dashboards. Turning one off does not turn off the others.

The PassionCode launcher, from version 0.2.76, sends anonymous usage and error counts: that it was installed or updated (with the version it was updated from), which of its commands ran, how it ended, roughly how long it took, and once a day that it was used. Each count carries the launcher’s version, the operating system’s name and kernel version, the system language, the Node.js version and the installation id from the file the PassionCode.ai apps share on that computer. It never sends a path, a name, an argument, a token or the text of an error. The counts go to analytics.passioncode.ai; events are kept for two years, the installation id is removed from them after 400 days, and your IP address is not stored. Turn them off for the launcher with npx @passioncode-ai/passioncode@latest analytics off, or set DO_NOT_TRACK=1. If you already said no in a PassionCode.ai app, the launcher keeps that answer.
