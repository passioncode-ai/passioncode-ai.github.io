01 / WHAT IT DOES

What it does

  • One home for identitiesThe messenger accounts and bots live in one service, and no other agent holds them.
  • Permissions per agentEach agent gets only what it needs: reading, searching, sending or managing a group.
  • A person approves sendsMessages wait for a person’s approval unless the owner has set a standing rule.

02 / WHO IT IS FOR

Who it is for

For organizations whose agents talk to people on Telegram and should not each hold their own account.

What you get

  • No bot tokens scattered across projects
  • A record of every message sent
  • One switch that stops all sending

03 / HOW IT IS ASSEMBLED

How a template
becomes your agent

The template holds what we learned running agents of this kind. Our engineer agent assembles it for your organization; the details come on request.

  1. 01

    We start from your processes

    You tell us what the agent should do. We look at the work it takes over, the systems it needs and who approves what.

  2. 02

    Our engineer agent assembles it

    It takes the template that fits, connects your systems with the access you grant, and sets your rules and spending limits.

  3. 03

    It runs where you choose

    On your machines or in your own cloud, with your keys. Each agent shows its state and spend, and a person approves what needs a person.
