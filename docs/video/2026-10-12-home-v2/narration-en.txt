Your agent works for a week. Then it falls apart. The wrong account. It forgets what it learned. And nobody can say what it changed. PassionCode.ai is the agent-agnostic operating system for AI-native teams: a workplace where agents do the work, and you see all of it. It runs on your machines, as open source, with the agents and subscriptions you already have. Their work leaves a record you can open. For you, it starts with one command: skills for Claude Code or Codex. No account, no key. Fabric, in preview, is the home for your projects and their agents. Your coding agent shows its plan before it changes anything. Switchboard: each agent on the account it should, limits in view. Dashboards: state, spend and control, in one window. Observatory: what changed, with the evidence. Inbox, in preview: mail your agents answer only within your rules. Agents in one project share its board, memory and hand-offs, so one picks up where another stopped. For an organization, we bring agents onto your machines, connect them, and show what each one cost and what it delivered. Agents are easy to start and hard to keep. We are building what holds them together.
