01 / AGENT SPRAWL

## Agents multiply faster

## than anyone can hold them

Every team can start an agent in an afternoon. Few can say a month later which agents run, on which accounts, what they cost, what they changed and whether anyone still needs them. Agents built alone do not talk to each other, and the ones nobody looks after quietly rot.

MCKINSEY · NOVEMBER 2025

### Many experiment, few scale

62% of organizations surveyed are at least experimenting with AI agents, yet in any single business function no more than 10% say they are scaling them.

GARTNER · JUNE 2025

### Many projects will stop

Gartner predicts that over 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by the end of 2027, because of rising costs, unclear business value or inadequate risk controls.

GARTNER · APRIL 2026

### Sprawl is the next problem

By 2028 an average global Fortune 500 enterprise will have over 150,000 agents in use, Gartner predicts, while only 13% of organizations think they have the right agent governance in place.

FORTUNE · AUGUST 2025

### The gap is learning, not models

A report from the NANDA initiative at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology found most generative AI pilots stalling with no measurable effect on profit and loss, and named the cause a learning gap: tools that do not adapt to the way a company works.

McKinsey, The state of AI in 2025: Agents, innovation, and transformation · November 2025

Gartner press release, 25 June 2025

Gartner press release, 28 April 2026

Fortune, 18 August 2025, on the report The GenAI Divide: State of AI in Business 2025. A caveat: the report rests on 150 interviews, a survey of 350 employees and a review of 300 public deployments, and its headline share of failing pilots has been questioned for how it defines success. Read it as a signal, not a measurement.

02 / THE MISSING HARNESS

## The model is not the problem

## What holds it is

A harness is everything around a model that makes an agent dependable over time: where it works, which tools and accounts it may use, what it remembers between sessions, the checks its work must pass, and the record of what it did. The labs that build the models use the same word: Anthropic writes about effective harnesses for long-running agents (November 2025), and OpenAI about harness engineering (February 2026).

01

### A home

Each project has one place for its purpose, board, decisions and releases, shared by every agent that works on it. That is Fabric.

02

### Accounts

Each agent runs on the account it should, with its limits in view. That is Fabric Switchboard.

03

### Health and control

Every agent service, its state, spend and updates, in one window that people and agents can drive. That is Fabric Dashboards.

04

### Memory and evidence

What changed, what was decided and what needs attention, with the proof beside it. That is Project Observatory.

One contract ties them together: the open Fabric Agent Contract. The Fabric Agent Adapter skill teaches any coding agent to build to it, so an agent you make today joins the family without a rewrite.

03 / WHAT WE BELIEVE

## Six principles

## we build by

Agents you ownThey run on your machines and your accounts, in code anyone can read under the GNU AGPL-3.0.

Any agentVendor-neutral. A harness on top of Claude Code, Codex and whatever comes next, never a replacement for them.

06 / TRUST

## What you can rely on

## at every stage

### Security

Each agent gets only the tools and accounts you allow. Keys stay in protected local storage; releases are signed and checked before they install.

### Data

Your work stays on your computers and your cloud accounts. Fabric and Switchboard send only anonymous usage counts, and you can turn them off. Privacy

### No lock-in

Open source under the GNU AGPL-3.0, an open contract, any coding agent and your own model accounts. Leave whenever you like and keep everything.

### Reliability

Every tool has a public test command and release notes, and says plainly what does not work yet. Done means checked against evidence.

### People

Analytics are about agents and outcomes: what ran, what it cost, what was approved. Nothing is stealth, and what is measured is visible to the people it concerns.

QUESTIONS

## Before you start

Does PassionCode.ai replace Claude Code or Codex?

No. It works on top of them. Your coding agent keeps doing the work; PassionCode.ai gives it a home, accounts, health, memory and evidence, and lets the next agent join the same family.

Is any of this a promise?

No. Each stage on this page is marked available now or direction. The tools listed as available can be downloaded today; the rest is where we are building, in that order.

Does PassionCode.ai score employees?

No. Analytics are about agents and the outcomes of processes: what ran, what it cost, what was approved. Nothing watches people in secret, and what is measured is visible to the people it concerns.
