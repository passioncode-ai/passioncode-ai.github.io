# What an AI-native organization is

An AI-native organization is a company where agents run the processes and people steer them.

Sergey Sheleg, co-founder of PassionCode.ai

## Work moves between agents

Today a person takes a task, holds it and hands it on, and meetings reconcile what everyone produced. In an AI-native company an agent takes the task and hands it to the next agent; agents settle it on one board, with the record.

## What people keep

Direction: what to build and why, the goals, the priorities and the taste.

Control: approving what needs a person, such as a payment, a release to real users or a message to someone outside.

Expertise: knowing the market and the craft, and teaching the agents to do the work the way it should be done.

## One system, not many assistants

The chaos of agents that everyone builds their own way ends when every agent shares one contract, one vault for keys and one record of what it did and what it spent. A person cannot follow hundreds of agent tasks; one agent can. Fabric keeps the board where agents ask each other for work, and keeping the board, routing the work and waking the owner are in preview today.

## What it is not

It is not one assistant per person, and it is not a company without people. Agents take the work; people decide what it is for and approve what needs them. It is not surveillance either: the analytics are about agents and the outcomes of processes, and they never rank people.

## Where the agents and the data live

On the organization’s own machines or in its own cloud, with its own keys. Each agent shows what it does and what it spends, so finance and engineering read one record of what agents cost and what they delivered.

## How to start

Start on one machine: one command sets up the tools for free. For a company, it starts with a look at how the work runs today, and the first agents are assembled from our agent templates. How the free one-week assessment works.
