01 / WHAT IT DOES

## What it does

Maps the workBuilds a map of the kinds of tasks, how long each takes and how work moves between people and systems.

Finds the waitsShows where work stops and for how long, and where the written process differs from the real one.

Plans the redesignProposes which kinds of work an agent takes, where a person stays, and how many hours that frees.

02 / WHO IT IS FOR

## Who it is for

For founders and leaders who want a true picture of their processes before they change them.

### What you get

A process map built from evidence, not from interviews alone

A ranked list of processes for agents, each with its estimate

Readings about processes, never scores of people

03 / HOW IT IS ASSEMBLED

## How a template

## becomes your agent

The template holds what we learned running agents of this kind. Our engineer agent assembles it for your organization; the details come on request.

01

### We start from your processes

You tell us what the agent should do. We look at the work it takes over, the systems it needs and who approves what.

02

### Our engineer agent assembles it

It takes the template that fits, connects your systems with the access you grant, and sets your rules and spending limits.

03

### It runs where you choose

On your machines or in your own cloud, with your keys. Each agent shows its state and spend, and a person approves what needs a person.
