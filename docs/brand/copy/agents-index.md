HOW IT WORKS

## How a template

## becomes your agent

Each template holds what we learned running agents of that kind in our own work. We do not hand out the template: we assemble it into your agent.

01

### We start from your processes

You tell us what the agent should do. We look at the work it takes over, the systems it needs and who approves what.

02

### Our engineer agent assembles it

It takes the template that fits, connects your systems with the access you grant, and sets your rules and spending limits.

03

On your machines or in your own cloud, with your keys. Each agent shows its state and spend, and a person approves what needs a person.

QUESTIONS

## Before you ask

Can I see a template’s code or prompts?

No. The templates are our own library. You receive the agent assembled for your organization, and we share the details of a template when we discuss your request.

Is a template a finished product?

No. A template is where we start: what we learned running agents of that kind. Each agent is assembled for your processes and checked on them before it starts work.

Where do the agents run?

On your machines or in your own cloud, with your keys. You see what each agent does and what it spends.

What does it cost?

There is no fixed package. We name the price after we understand your processes, and the estimate comes before any contract.
