01 / WHAT IT DOES

## What it does

Scenarios in a browserRuns a scenario written in plain words and proves the result.

Pages and checkoutsWalks every page looking for errors and checks that every payment form opens.

Incidents, not noiseTurns error and metric alerts into incidents without duplicates, and starts a repair.

02 / WHO IT IS FOR

## Who it is for

For teams that ship often and want to find problems before their users report them.

### What you get

Bugs found before users find them

Reported bugs checked against production

Known vulnerabilities matched to what you ship

03 / HOW IT IS ASSEMBLED

## How a template

## becomes your agent

The template holds what we learned running agents of this kind. Our engineer agent assembles it for your organization; the details come on request.

01

### We start from your processes

You tell us what the agent should do. We look at the work it takes over, the systems it needs and who approves what.

02

### Our engineer agent assembles it

It takes the template that fits, connects your systems with the access you grant, and sets your rules and spending limits.

03

### It runs where you choose

On your machines or in your own cloud, with your keys. Each agent shows its state and spend, and a person approves what needs a person.
