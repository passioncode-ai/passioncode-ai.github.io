01 / WHAT IT DOES

## What it does

One book of factsFinance, metrics, team, product and any block you add, each fact with where it came from.

Access by levelPeople and agents read the blocks their key opens, and nothing about one person leaves.

Never invents a numberA missing value stays missing: it is never filled with a guess or a zero.

02 / WHO IT IS FOR

## Who it is for

For organizations whose numbers live in many spreadsheets and disagree with each other.

### What you get

One answer when someone asks for a number

Agents that quote facts with their sources

Reports for the board and investors from one place

03 / HOW IT IS ASSEMBLED

## How a template

## becomes your agent

The template holds what we learned running agents of this kind. Our engineer agent assembles it for your organization; the details come on request.

01

### We start from your processes

You tell us what the agent should do. We look at the work it takes over, the systems it needs and who approves what.

02

### Our engineer agent assembles it

It takes the template that fits, connects your systems with the access you grant, and sets your rules and spending limits.

03

### It runs where you choose

On your machines or in your own cloud, with your keys. Each agent shows its state and spend, and a person approves what needs a person.
