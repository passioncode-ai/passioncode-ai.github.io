# SEO / AEO build-time brief: the site after Humanless (SITE-079)

- **Date:** 2026-11-21
- **What is being built:** new pages of a live site and changes to five live ones: the Vision home, Fabric and its
  CEO agent, a page per part of the product and per feature, the organizations' page with three cases, «Assess your
  company», pricing, and the pain guides. Spec: [SITE-079](../tasks/2026-11-21-humanless-site/README.md)
- **Markets / languages:** the site's ten languages (`i18n/locales.json`): English at `/`, then `/ru/`, `/de/`,
  `/fr/`, `/pl/`, `/ko/`, `/es/`, `/pt-br/`, `/zh-hans/`, `/ja/`; every new page exists in each. The pain guides also
  take the guide-only languages of `i18n/scoped-locales.json`
- **Live today:** the rest of passioncode.ai; the brief of 2026-11-08 covers `/agents/`, `/guides/` and `/blog/` and
  stays in force for them
- **Owners:** copy · this repository's agent (English source, catalogs) with Copylot · design: the design system
  (`styles.css`, `design-system/`) through sheleg-design · engineering: `scripts/pages.mjs`,
  `scripts/build-content-pages.mjs`, `scripts/locales.mjs`, the Worker · analytics: `docs/analytics/events.md`

This brief records decisions, not findings. Nothing here is observed yet; each row names the audit section that will
verify it after launch. A row the project does not need stays, marked `n/a` with a reason. Search volume was not
measured for any URL below, so the table carries no volume column.

Written with the `seo-aeo-audit` skill's brief mode (`references/build-time.md`, version 0.32.5) before the pages are
built (owner, 2026-11-21: «Подумать качественную SEO-структуру»).

Cannibalization decided in the plan, before anything is written:

- `/assessment/` (start it) · `/guides/free-assessment/` (how the week works) · `/agents/company-assessment/` (what
  the agent template is) · `assessment.passioncode.ai` (the story, another host). Each keeps its own question and
  links to the other three by what they answer.
- `/` (what it frees you from) · `/vision/` (how it grows) · `/fabric/` (what the product is): the home carries no
  second copy of the vision's stages or of Fabric's surfaces, only one screen and a link each.
- `/start/` (the command) · `/launcher/` (what the command installs) · `/guides/start-agent-workplace/` (what happens
  at each step): only `/start/` carries the command as its action.
- `/fabric/agents/` is the agent-agnostic page; no `/features/agent-agnostic/` is built.
- `/open-source/` (the licence in plain words) · `/pricing/` (what is paid) · `/business/` (the commercial licence
  request): the price lives on `/pricing/` only, the licence text link on `/open-source/` only.
- `/guides/why-releases-slip/` (releases) · `/guides/catch-process-failures/` (any process): the new guide takes the
  general question and links to the release guide as its worked case.
- `/business/humanless-business/` · `/blog/what-is-an-ai-native-organization/`: the case page is commercial (what we
  do for your business), the post informational (what the term means).
- `/fabric/create/` (how creating works in Fabric) · `/agents/` (which agents exist): the feature page links to the
  catalogue and lists no templates of its own.

## Do not build

| Idea | Why not |
|---|---|
| A `/features/` hub with a second copy of each feature | features live with what they belong to (`/fabric/<name>/` or the root); `/fabric/` is their hub |
| A page for Fabric VR, PassionView, Vesta, the platform or the agents database | private or without a release (spec D12) |
| A page per pain on the home, or a «problems» section beside the guides | the guides answer those questions already (spec D5) |
| A price for Organization, Enterprise, the cloud instance or services before the owners set it | spec D9; the page says «by offer after the assessment» |
| A valuation or sale-price figure on the sale-ready case | no measured figure exists |
| `FAQPage` markup, `llms.txt` as a ranking lever, a date bump without a content change, pages made for volume | the skill's myth list |
| Content that exists only inside the chat or behind the rabbit's animation | brief N3 |
| A redirect or rename of a live URL | brief N1 |
| A new contact form | the request on `/business/#request` stays the one form; the chat sends the same contract |

`/assessment/`, `/pricing/` and the three case pages are landing pages: after they are built, run super-ux's
readiness check on the built page (answers in the markup, one action one label, the argument without JavaScript, one

`psi_pull.py` against the live URL. A page with no field data yet says so; a lab score does not stand in for it.
