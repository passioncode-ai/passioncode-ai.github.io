Your agent works for a week. Then it runs on the wrong account, forgets what it learned, and nobody can say what it changed. It needs a home, its accounts, its memory and a record of what it did. Fabric is the home for your projects and their agents, in preview. It opens on four actions: create or adapt an agent, open or create a project. Your coding agent asks its questions and shows its plan first. Each agent runs on the account it should. Its dashboard and spend sit in one window, and Observatory shows what changed. The tools are available today. Coordination between agents is the direction we are building.
