How agent templates become your agents

We do not build your agents from zero. Each one starts from a template that holds what we learned running agents of that kind, and is assembled for your processes.

Sergey Sheleg, co-founder of PassionCode.ai

What a template is

A template describes one kind of agent: the job it does, the rules it keeps, what it must never do and when it calls a person, with tested code behind it. It collects what the agents of that kind we run in our own work have learned. See the agent templates.

From your request to an agent

You tell us what agents should do. Our engineer agent picks the template that fits, connects your systems with the access you grant, sets your rules and spending limits, and checks the agent on your own processes before it starts work.

Where it runs

On your machines or in your own cloud, with your keys. Each agent shows its state and what it spends, and a person approves what needs a person: a payment, a release to real users, a message to someone outside.

What stays yours

Your data, your keys and your agents. The template library stays ours: you receive the assembled agents, not the library, and we share the details of a template when we discuss your request.

Agents that get better

When we improve a template, the improvement can reach your agent with the template’s next release, after the checks your agent already passes.
