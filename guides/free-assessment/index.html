How the free one-week assessment works

One agent reads how your company works for a week, with read-only access. At the end you get a map of how work really runs and a ready offer for what agents should take over first.

Sergey Sheleg, co-founder of PassionCode.ai

ON REQUEST

Before the week

You answer four questions: your goal, the size of your team, the systems you use and what hurts most. You give read-only access to those systems, one link each. Our engineer agent sets the assessment agent up in a space of its own for your organization, or on your own machine if no data should leave it. This takes about twenty minutes of your time.

During the week

The agent connects to your trackers, repositories, documents, team chats, mail and support, and reads the last 90 days. Then it follows the work as it happens: what was planned and what happened, where work waits and for how long. If the data leaves gaps, it may ask your team a few short questions in the channel they already use, anonymous by default. Nothing changes in your systems.

What you get on day seven

  • A map of your processes: the kinds of tasks, how much time each takes and how work moves between people and systems.
  • Plan against fact, the blockers and waits, and where the documented process differs from the real one.
  • The offer: the processes agents should take over first, the hours each frees, the agent template that does it, and a pilot on one process to prove it.

We go through it with you in a 30-minute call, and you keep it as a page.

About people, never scores

The assessment reads processes, not people. It reports totals for groups, never a score for a person, and what stays with people (direction, control and expertise) is part of the offer.

After the week

If you go ahead, the pilot starts in the same place. If not, access is revoked, the data is deleted, and you receive a confirmation that it was.
