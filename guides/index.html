Guides to working with agents

Short guides to setting up the tools on your computer and to bringing agents into your organization

Each guide explains what happens at each step before you take it. They help two kinds of readers: people who set up the tools on their own computer, and leaders who bring agents into a company.

Where to start

  • On your own computer: read the setup guide first, then run the command from the Get started page.
  • For your company: read how the free one-week assessment works, then how agent templates become your agents.
